# Community Service Project (CSP) Report Summary
## Project Title: Smart Complaint Management System for Rural Areas
**Target Village:** Venkatapuram Gram Panchayat, Andhra Pradesh  
**Academic Program:** B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Community Service Project)  

---

### 1. Village Profile & Demographics
- **Village Name:** Venkatapuram
- **Gram Panchayat:** Venkatapuram Gram Panchayat
- **Mandal / District:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Approximate Population:** ~3,400 residents across 6 Wards (Main Bazaar, BC Colony, SC Colony, Temple Street, Agricultural Quarters, School Junction).
- **Local Governance:** Sarpanch, Panchayat Secretary, and Ward Members.

---

### 2. Civic Problems Identified During Field Survey
During the door-to-door community survey and interaction with residents in Venkatapuram, the following six major issues were recorded:

1. **Drinking Water Scarcity & Pipeline Leaks:**
   - Irregular timing of drinking water supply from the local RO plant.
   - Underground pipe bursts along Temple Street causing water contamination and road slush.
2. **Damaged Link Roads & Potholes:**
   - The 2.4 km stretch connecting Venkatapuram to the main Mandal Road has deep potholes, causing frequent two-wheeler accidents and hindering auto-rickshaw access during monsoon.
3. **Faulty Streetlights & Dark Spots:**
   - 18+ non-functional sodium/LED streetlights across BC Colony and near Zilla Parishad High School, posing safety concerns for women and school children after dark.
4. **Drainage Overflow & Silt Accumulation:**
   - Uncovered side drains clogged with plastic waste and silt, leading to foul odor and mosquito breeding (dengue/malaria risk).
5. **Garbage Collection Irregularities:**
   - Solid waste collection tractor visits only once or twice a week, forcing residents to dump domestic waste in open vacant plots on the village outskirts.
6. **Electricity Fluctuations & Exposed Cables:**
   - Low voltage issues affecting agricultural pump sets and domestic appliances during evening hours, along with sagging electrical cables near field borewells.

---

### 3. Objectives of the Smart Complaint Management System
1. **Digital Empowerment:** Provide a user-friendly online portal for rural citizens to lodge civic grievances anytime, anywhere without having to repeatedly travel to the Panchayat office.
2. **Transparent Tracking:** Every grievance receives a unique tracking number (e.g., `SCM-2026-0001`) with live status updates and a timestamped progress timeline.
3. **Accountability & Speed:** Enable the Sarpanch and Panchayat Secretary to assign staff, set priorities (Low / Medium / High), add official remarks, and reduce grievance resolution times to under 72 hours.
4. **Bilingual Accessibility:** Deliver full support in both **Telugu (తెలుగు)** and **English** so every villager, irrespective of literacy barrier, can submit and track complaints comfortably.
5. **Data-Driven Governance:** Provide graphical reports and charts for Panchayat administrators to identify chronic problem zones and allocate development funds effectively.
